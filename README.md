# The [_Book of Blessings_](http://gfassero.github.io/book-of-blessings/)
The [_Book of Blessings_](http://gfassero.github.io/book-of-blessings/), published by the authority of Pope John Paul II, approved for use in the USA and confirmed by Rome, now available (unofficially) on your phone. The _Standard Book of Spells_ for Catholics. Now you can bless your kids, your Advent wreath, your neighbor’s car, the parish swim team, the world.

The web app is available at http://gfassero.github.io/book-of-blessings/.

## Who can use it?
Most of these blessings can be given by any baptized person. A few are reserved to clergy.

## How to use it
- Read the [guide to using the app](http://gfassero.github.io/book-of-blessings/guide.html).
- Read the [General Introduction to the _Book of Blessings_](http://gfassero.github.io/book-of-blessings/front-21.html).

## Sources
The text is taken from the 1992 [_Book of Blessings, Abridged Edition_](http://archive.org/details/bookofblessingsa0000cath) and the 1990 [_Shorter Book of Blessings_](http://archive.org/details/shorterbookofble0000cath). Where these editions differ slightly, I’ve chosen the one that seems more consistent with the other the blessings in the book. I chose not to use the full-length _Book of Blessings_ because it includes many blessings that are intended for use during Mass or with greater solemnity, in which case, you should be reading from the book, not your phone.

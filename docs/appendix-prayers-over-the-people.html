These blessings are intended for use by a priest or deacon.

Prayers Over the People

1277 After the prayer over the people the priest or deacon always adds:

May almighty God bless you,
the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit.

℟. Amen.

1278 Prayers for General Use

1 Lord,
have mercy on your people.
Grant us in this life the good things
that lead to the everlasting life you prepare for us.

We ask this through Christ our Lord.

℟. Amen.

May almighty God bless you,
the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit.

℟. Amen.

2 Lord,
grant your people your protection and grace.
Give them health of mind and body,
perfect love for one another,
and make them always faithful to you.

Grant this through Christ our Lord.

℟. Amen.

May almighty God bless you,
the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit.

℟. Amen.

1279 Prayers for Use on the Feasts of Saints

Give us fellowship with them
and unending joy in your kingdom.

We ask this in the name of Jesus the Lord.

℟. Amen.

May almighty God bless you,
the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit.

℟. Amen.

you have given us many friends in heaven.

and fill our hearts with your love.

Grant this through Christ our Lord.

℟. Amen.

May almighty God bless you,
the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit.

℟. Amen.

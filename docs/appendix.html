Introduction

1273 The following solemn blessings and prayers over the people may be used to conclude any of the blessings contained in this book. They may also be used for the blessing at the end of Morning or Evening Prayer of the Liturgy of the Hours or on any occasion when a priest or deacon is asked to give a blessing.
